GenLayer Spinner Portal mission 12

A loading state worth seeing a thousand times.

An original spinner for the GenLayer Portal. The resting track and the GenLayer mark inherit currentColor, so one file works on every surface. The sweep is the GenLayer Points rainbow, sampled from the live portal. Ships as a standalone SVG, a pure CSS build, and a React component.

Legibility

Holds its shape from 96px down to 16px

Stroke weight is tied to diameter at a fixed ratio, so the ring never turns into a hairline or a blob. Below 24px the mark is dropped and the silhouette carries the identity, which is the honest call at that size instead of pretending a 6px logo is readable.

Family

One primary, two siblings

The primary spinner covers every general loading state. The two siblings exist for the moments that deserve a beat of their own.

Primary

Consensus

The default. A rainbow arc sweeps the ring while the mark holds centre and pulses once as the arc closes, the beat where a decision lands.

Variant

Quorum

Three validators orbit and fire in turn, and the mark brightens once all three have spoken. For validator and consensus screens.

Variant

Sweep

A band of the rainbow rises through the mark and loops without a seam. For first paint and full page transitions where the logo can take the stage.

Install

Three ways in

<!-- 1. standalone SVG, animates inside an img tag -->
<img src="genlayer-spinner.svg" width="40" height="40" alt="Loading">
<!-- 2. pure CSS, one element, inherits color -->
<link rel="stylesheet" href="genlayer-spinner.css">
<span class="gl-spinner" role="status" aria-label="Loading"></span>
<span class="gl-spinner gl-spinner--sm"></span>
<span class="gl-spinner" style="--gl-size:72px;--gl-duration:1.9s"></span>
// 3. React
import GenLayerSpinner from "./GenLayerSpinner";

<GenLayerSpinner size={40} />
<GenLayerSpinner size={16} showMark={false} />
